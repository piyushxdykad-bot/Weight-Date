About the gym

The Weight Date is a strength family from Prayagraj. Two floors of serious iron, coaches who write your plan for free, and a powerlifting team that turns up to district meets and brings the medals home. Beginners start here. Champions train here. Everyone keeps the date.

Why red? On a competition bumper plate, red means 25 kilograms. It is the heaviest plate on the bar, and it is our colour.

Facilities

Not a single weak machine.

Each floor is split into zones, so a powerlifter, a first-timer and a cardio regular can all train at peak hour without waiting on each other.

Zone 01 · Powerlifting

The platform

Racks, benches and deadlift platforms set up for the three competition lifts. This is where our 200 kg club pulls and our district medallists train their meet prep.

  • Squat racks
  • Competition benches
  • Deadlift platforms
  • Calibrated plates
Squat · Bench · Deadlift
Zone 02 · Free weights

The dumbbell wall

Our 40 kg pairs were upgraded to VIVA Tunturi TPU dumbbells, commercial grade and built for heavy daily use. Cable stations carry VIVA mug-handle grips.

  • VIVA Tunturi TPU dumbbells
  • Mug-handle grips
  • Adjustable benches
Up to 40 kg pairs
Zone 03 · Cardio

The cardio deck

A dedicated line of treadmills and bikes for warm-ups, conditioning blocks and long fat-loss sessions, away from the lifting traffic.

  • Treadmills
  • Bikes
  • Separate zone
Conditioning · Fat loss
Zone 04 · Functional

The open floor

Open space for circuits, mobility, core work and the challenges you see on our page, like the invisible chair.

  • Kettlebells
  • Mobility space
  • Circuit training
